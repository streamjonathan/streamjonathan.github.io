# Terms of use

This is a personal, non-commercial website, run by Jonathan McCallum. There is nothing for sale here, no advertising, and no tracking. These terms are short, in plain language.

## The writing

Everything here is Jonathan's own writing and his own views, unless a post says otherwise. You are welcome to read it, share a link to it, and quote a short part of it with a link back. Please do not copy whole articles elsewhere, and do not present his writing as your own.

## The chat

The chat ("Ask the writing") is an AI, not Jonathan. It answers only from Jonathan's published writing, and it can get things wrong. It is not Jonathan, and it does not speak for him. Please do not share personal details in the chat. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for how questions are handled.

## Comments

Comments show here once Jonathan has had a look at them. To comment you sign in with Google, Instagram, LINE or email, with your real name — no anonymous comments, no made-up names.

Comments are not accepted if they are spam, abusive, or share someone else's personal details. If a comment is removed, Jonathan will say why when he replies. To report a comment, write to Jonathan at <a href="#" data-contact-email data-contact-email-text>Email me</a>.

## Notes and stories

Notes are anonymous. If you share your story, nothing is published without Jonathan first checking it and talking with you. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for how notes and stories are kept.

## Under 18

This site is not for people under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use the chat, notes or comments, and do not write to Jonathan.

## No professional advice

Nothing here is professional advice of any kind — not medical, legal, financial or otherwise. If something on this site is wrong or out of date, tell Jonathan and he will put it right.

These terms may change if the site changes. The privacy page is at [Privacy](/privacy/). Last updated 25 November 2026.
