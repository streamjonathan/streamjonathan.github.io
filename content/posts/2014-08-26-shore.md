Shade spreads out across a speckled shore

A trillion fragments of seashells farewell the orange light

Blue sea bright, deepening to green with dusk

Sea birds nestled near, fish at play

Dusk falls to night

Sea shimmering in the sparkling silver starlights

Wind lifts salty air to my lips, I gaze, I stare

Then

Sun spreads out across a speckled shore….
