The disciples wanted to know how to pray. Jesus gave them a short prayer.

Jesus was teaching his disciples on the mountain. He told them not to pray with many words to be heard. God already knows what we need before we ask.

So he gave them these words to pray: "Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: for thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen."

That is the whole prayer: bread, forgiveness, and help.

What do you notice about how short this prayer is?
