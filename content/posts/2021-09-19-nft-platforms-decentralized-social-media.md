Here are some of the NFT platforms I’ve been looking into with their associated blockchain currency:

Nafter (Nafter), BitClout (Clout), PeakD (Hive), Atomic Hubs (Wax), Hicetnunc (Tezos) is a Decentralized Social Media

Today I’ll just be focusing on Hicetnunc which is latin for “here and now”

[Hicetnunc.xyz](https://www.hicetnunc.xyz/streamjonathan/creations) is where the art seen below has been minted. Hicetnunc uses the effeciency and low energy consumption / cost of the Tezos blockchain and has been an excellent community for both artists and collectors alike. The lower priced artwork along with the very low gas fee (transaction costs) have enabled artists to also become collectors within this community and it seems the other Tezos based platforms are also growing.
