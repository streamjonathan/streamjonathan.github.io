On the night before he died, Jesus prayed out loud, with his friends listening. He prayed for them — and for all who would believe.

And in the middle of it he said something strange: "Father, glorify me in your presence with the glory I had with you before the world began."

Before the world began. Before there was anything at all, he was there, with the Father, sharing his glory. Then he came here, to us. And now he was asking to go home.

He did not claim a glory he would earn one day. He spoke of a glory he already had, before time.

What was he like, before the world began? Why would he leave that glory and come here? And what does it mean that he prayed for you?
