When I look back at my childhood, those books are key to my faith journey, following Jesus, understanding who Jesus is.

People who don't like Lewis's portrayal of God as Aslan don't much like talking to me about God. Lewis influenced me so much I wanted to know who Jesus is after reading Narnia — the magic of knowing God through a story, then reading what he is actually like in the scriptures, in Jesus.

Aslan is so good it's impossible to portray him in a way that fully explains what Lewis was going for. Lewis did the best he could to explain who God is.

Narnia isn't an allegory — it's imagining an alternative circumstance. A world where Jesus is a lion. I did enjoy the BBC version — the French horn captures my heart every time I hear it. If you haven't read Lewis, try The Great Divorce, about heaven, and The Space Trilogy — the first two books are fantastic.

This is NOT a prediction if I will, or if you will, like or appreciate the movie. I'm just happy we are talking about faith in Jesus because of these books by Lewis. Greta Gerwig's Narnia: The Magician's Nephew is coming — cinemas Feb 2027, Netflix in April. I made a video about the new film, Lewis and the nature of God. You can watch it here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LOAuNNf0eVs

Any movie trying to adapt Narnia will do a good or a bad job. Yes, I will watch the movie. Maybe I'll skip over parts. Maybe it will rekindle my interest in the beauty of Jesus. Maybe there will be parts I disagree with. Yet I want the conversation to lean back into Jesus. And I understand if you think a wrong portrayal in this movie could turn people away from who Jesus is — that is always a risk with movies, or any retelling. I reckon Jesus is bigger than all of this though, and He is reaching out to people. I'll probably be thinking of the first time I picked up The Magician's Nephew — a book that moved so many hearts around the world. That's why they're loved so much. Sticking to the source material is a great idea.

It's up to you and me to discern what is true and good. Keep on following Jesus. Look for him wherever you go and lift up other people.
