*Water Lilies and Japanese Bridge, Claude Monet (1899), public domain.*

full life

through my frosted glass mind,

you see, me. that’s all it takes,

to melt the relentless self-ness. your spirit river

flowing, seeing,

filling this familiar empty well,

with love.

why is it that when i see you,

i feel freedom-love,

fullness of life.

you’re a mystery unable to be contained

in my fumbling feebleness.
