My week was particularly dreary. I was feeling sick, and I noticed I hadn't been able to grab my surfboard and catch waves the way I like to. Today was not really an exception in the way I could surf. It was an exception in how the ocean and the sandbank aligned. The sun was flowing and the sandbank was glowing, and it just filled me with joy.

I am not really trying to make up a poem here. I am just trying to express some of the things I enjoyed so much about the day. The rabbit is sitting there. I need to make dinner. And I am thinking about the darkness of the week, where the ocean is a picture of the waves of emotions, of feeling negative, and wondering why people suffer so much.

![Jonathan talking into a microphone with his hand raised](/images/suffering-surfing-and-jesus.jpg)

In all of my videos I have had this problem in my mind. Yes, things are good. However, things are also incredibly difficult for many, many people on this planet. It is a theological discussion that I think is very worth our time.

I am only going to give you a personal experience of my week, and I hope it creates thoughts and ideas, in the comments and in conversations. Things are not perfect. They are broken. And yet I sense that God is mending and healing, not only in my personal life. I pray that is something I can keep having the faith to see.

God reaches out to each and every person, offering grace and mercy and forgiveness. Not stingily, but in great generosity. I see that in Jesus, in how he lives and walks. And I hope you would feel held and close to God in this very day, in this very moment.
