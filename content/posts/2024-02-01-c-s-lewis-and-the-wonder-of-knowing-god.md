There is a verse from Paul I keep coming back to. He said, I decided while I was with you, I would forget everything except Jesus Christ. And the amazing part is that while he has forgotten everything, he has actually found everything.

No eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no mind has imagined what God has prepared for those who love him. I made this video mostly to remind myself of that truth.

![Jonathan talking about C S Lewis and the wonder of knowing God](/images/c-s-lewis-and-the-wonder-of-knowing-god.jpg)

Paul wrote that he did not use lofty words or impressive wisdom. He came in weakness, and his message was very plain. He relied only on the power of the Holy Spirit, so they would not trust in human wisdom but in the power of God.

That is where I want to jump into C. S. Lewis's book, The Last Battle. If you are familiar with it, you will know the scene. The main characters have died. It is the end of the world and the start of the new world, which becomes the start of eternity. They get to go on in the great story, further up and further in, closer to Aslan, closer into his country.

They go through a garden of Eden sort of place. They find a world within a world, a world that is becoming more real. I will leave that idea there, because if you are reading it, it sparks the imagination of how the good things of earth become real in a way we could never fathom.

And it is in tremendous running into love that happens in that last scene. They are running toward love himself, Jesus. And there are others running in that same direction from different worlds and different places.

My videos are very much unscripted and from my heart. I stumble over the words sometimes, especially at the end, because I am overwhelmed by how good God's plan is. For us now, we get a taste of heaven in the renewing of our life, and our imagination is captivated by Jesus himself.
