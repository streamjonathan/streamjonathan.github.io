We all know what it feels like to be passed by when we need help.

A man going down from Jerusalem to Jericho was attacked by robbers. They stripped him, beat him, and left him half dead. A priest came by, saw him, and passed by on the other side. A Levite did the same.

Then a Samaritan came. He saw the man, and he had pity on him. He bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine, put him on his own animal, and brought him to an inn. The next day he gave the innkeeper two denarii and said, "Take care of him. Whatever more you spend, I will repay you when I come back."

"Which of these three was a neighbour?" Jesus asked. "The one who showed him mercy." "Go and do likewise," Jesus said.

Who is the neighbour you would least expect? When have you been the one passed by — or the one passing by? And what would it cost you to stop and help?
