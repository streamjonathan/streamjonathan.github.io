This is a very personal thought. When we think about Jesus as God, Jesus speaks. The people who follow him listen. Some people do not listen. And some follow him but do not really listen, even though they say they do.

I think about how we can be different. God's Spirit changes us. But sometimes it seems to me that people look down on us, as if to say, I have got it, so you should follow my example. Most often I find support, but sometimes it feels that way.

![Jonathan talking about being like Jesus](/images/be-like-jesus-and-not-proud-nor-loud-nor-over-confident.jpg)

Not everyone who is loud or confident actually lives their faith. Sincerity and authenticity are proved over time. That may sound critical, but I think it is true.

My understanding of faith is that Jesus transforms us through his Holy Spirit. It has a lot to do with shaping our hearts, quieting our minds, and loving in the middle of suffering. Loving others even when people do not treat you the way you want them to. Loving when people have been offended, hurt, or harmed.

Quiet faith looks like listening before speaking. It looks like not needing to win the argument, and letting the Spirit do the shaping over time. The loudest voice in the room is not always the one that has been changed the most.

I think our examples in the Christian faith are the people who are able to keep loving, keep hoping, and keep believing that God will eventually work things out, instead of seeking revenge, instead of wishing evil or harm on their enemy. There are many of them.

Who has shown you what a quiet faith looks like?
