We all know what it feels like to want to sort things out right now — to pull up what does not belong.

Jesus said the kingdom of heaven is like a man who sowed good seed in his field. But while his men were sleeping, his enemy came and sowed weeds among the wheat and went away.

When the plants came up and bore grain, then the weeds appeared also. The servants came to the master and said, "Master, did you not sow good seed in your field? How then does it have weeds?"

He said, "An enemy has done this."

So the servants said, "Then do you want us to go and gather them?" But he said, "No, lest in gathering the weeds you root up the wheat along with them. Let both grow together until the harvest, and at harvest time I will tell the reapers, Gather the weeds first and bind them in bundles to be burned, but gather the wheat into my barn."

Why do you think he said to wait? What would happen if the weeds were pulled up now? And what does it mean that the harvest is the time for sorting?
