Philip said to Jesus, "Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us."

Jesus answered, "Don't you know me, Philip, even after I have been among you such a long time? Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father."

He had lived with them, eaten with them, walked with them for three years. And still they asked to see God.

Jesus said: look at me. What I do, the Father does. What I say, the Father says.

He did not tell them to look somewhere else. He told them to look at him.

What do you think God is like?
