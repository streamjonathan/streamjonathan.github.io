You began to shine well before you arrived from Planet Ork. 
 The day you were born, did we know you would bring so much joy to the world? 
 So many roles that would lead us and feed our avid appetite for humor?

You were our teacher in Dead Poets Society 
 Our dad and our leader in Hook 
 Our wish-granter in Aladdin 
 Our nanny in Mrs. Doubtfire 
 Our counselor in Good Will Hunting 
 Our doctor in Patch Adams

As we were growing, you were there 
 Encouraging, leading, laughing, pulling faces 
 Always uplifting

You have been and will be 
 A star that shines 
 Resting in peace 
 Among friends and the Friend who gave the world laughter.
