Everyone knows what it is like to walk in the dark. You move slowly. You reach out with your hands. You are never quite sure what is in front of you.

Light changes everything. It shows you where to put your feet. It also shows you what is really there — the good and the bad.

Jesus said, "I am the light of the world." He did not say He could show us the light, or that He knew where the light was. He said He was the light.

That is a large claim. Light is not one thing among many. Without light, you cannot see anything at all. Jesus was saying that without Him, we cannot see life as it really is — or find our way through it.

Lewis saw the same choice here as everywhere else in Jesus's words. A man who says he is the light of the world is either telling the truth, or he is not in his right mind. A good teacher would never say such a thing about himself.

If He is the light, then following Him is not about trying harder in the dark. It is about walking in the light He gives — and letting it show us the way, one step at a time.

What would change if you stopped feeling your way through the dark, and walked in the light instead?
