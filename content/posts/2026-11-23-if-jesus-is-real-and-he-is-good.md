I know this is being translated, and I hope it will be helpful and interesting as we talk about the most wonderful person — and more than a person. This is for my friends in Poland.

I first met Jesus through a story. I read the Narnia books when I was young, and I loved Aslan. He was not safe. But he was good. And I wanted to know if there was someone real like that.

He was good in a way that did not feel safe. He was kind to people others walked past. He ate with them. He forgave them. That is the kind of goodness I wanted to find in real life, not just in a story.

That is what brought me to Jesus. He is not just a good teacher. He is the one who gives life. He said he is the bread of life — the food that really fills us. I have found that to be true. When I am tired, or unsure, or far from home, he is still there.

I still think about Aslan when I read about Jesus. A lion who is not safe, but good. A king who lays down his life for others. That is who Jesus is to me — not an idea, but a person.

The books were the beginning. Jesus himself was the rest. I have not found a better story since.

I am an average person. I surf. I make videos. I am learning languages, and I make mistakes. But this is the most important thing I have found, and I wanted to share it with you first.

So here is my question for you. If Jesus is real, and he is good, what would change for you? You do not have to have it all worked out to ask the question. I did not. I would love to hear what you think.
