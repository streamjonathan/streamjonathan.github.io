We all know what it feels like to lose someone we love.

Jesus went to a city called Nain, with his disciples and a great crowd. At the gate, a dead man was being carried out — the only son of his mother, and she was a widow. A large crowd was with her.

When Jesus saw her, he had compassion on her. He said to her, "Weep not."

Then he came and touched the bier. Those carrying it stood still. And he said, "Young man, I say unto thee, Arise."

The dead man sat up and began to speak. And Jesus delivered him to his mother.

Fear came on all of them, and they glorified God, saying, "A great prophet is risen up among us," and, "God hath visited his people." And this news went out through all Judea and the country around.

What do you think the widow felt when Jesus said, "Weep not"? Why do you think Jesus stopped the funeral? And what does it say about him that he gave the son back to his mother?
