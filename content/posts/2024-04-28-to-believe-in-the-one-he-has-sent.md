There's a moment where Jesus is with his closest followers, and a crowd of people have just been fed by him, miraculously. They are interested in what he is going to do next. Instead of pushing them away, Jesus tells them to seek the food that lasts forever. The bread that lasts forever. He is talking about himself.

![Jonathan smiling by the sea, with the rocky shore behind him](/images/to-believe-in-the-one-he-has-sent.jpg)

That has stayed with me. What does it mean to believe in the one he has sent? Belief is not just a way of thinking it all through. It is more like showing love towards God and towards others.

I think belief does have a lot to do with the mind. But I saw a mountain bike parked just there, and it made me think. A bicycle only carries you somewhere when you put it into action. Belief is like that too. It moves when you live it.

And love is somehow an essential part of all kinds of faith in Jesus. Love is the power behind that belief. Jesus goes on to say that he is the bread of life. He is the water of life. If we drink and eat of him, we will never thirst again.

He doesn't give us a theological definition of what it means to believe in him. He calls himself the author and the sustainer of life. No matter what your background is, or wherever you are reading this, that is the invitation.

What would change if belief was something you lived, not just something you thought?
