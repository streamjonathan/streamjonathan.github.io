Jesus was telling his friends he was going away, to prepare a place for them. Thomas said, "Lord, we don't know where you are going, so how can we know the way?"

Jesus answered, "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."

He did not say he knew the way. He said he was the way. Not a map, not a set of directions — a person.

He did not point to the road. He said he was the road.

What road are you on? What would it mean if the way to God is a person? And where do you think he is taking you?
