"I am the true vine," Jesus said, "and my Father is the gardener." He said this to his friends on the night before he died.

A branch cannot bear fruit by itself. It has to stay joined to the vine. Cut off, it withers.

"I am the vine; you are the branches," he said. "If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing."

The gardener prunes the branches that bear fruit, so they bear even more.

He did not say try harder. He said stay connected.

What are you joined to? What fruit is growing in your life? And what would it mean to stay in him?
