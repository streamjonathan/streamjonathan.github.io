We all know what it feels like to be ignored when we ask for help.

Jesus told this story so his disciples would always pray and not give up. There was a judge in a certain town who neither feared God nor cared what people thought. And there was a widow in that town who kept coming to him and saying, "Give me justice against my adversary."

For a long time the judge refused. But at last he said to himself, "Even though I don't fear God or care what people think, I will see that she gets justice, because this widow keeps bothering me."

Then Jesus said, "Listen to what the unjust judge says. And will not God bring about justice for his chosen ones, who cry out to him day and night? He will see that they get justice, and quickly."

What do you keep asking God for? What would it mean to keep asking and not give up? And what does Jesus' question mean — when he comes, will he find faith on earth?
