Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon's porch. The leaders gathered around him and said, "How long will you keep us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly."

Jesus answered, "I did tell you, but you do not believe. The works I do in my Father's name testify about me."

Then he said it plainly: "I and the Father are one."

The leaders picked up stones again. They said he was blaspheming — making himself God. Jesus did not take it back.

He said who he was, plainly. And they understood exactly what he meant.

What do you make of that claim? Why do you think they reached for stones?
