Jesus said, "I am the door of the sheep."

In those days a sheepfold had one opening. At night the shepherd himself would lie down in the doorway — he was the door. Thieves climb in some other way. But the sheep come in through the door.

"Whoever enters through me will be saved," Jesus said. "They will come in and go out, and find pasture."

He did not say the way in was a rule, or a ritual, or a test. He said the way in was him.

What are you looking for a way into? What does it mean that the way in is a person, not a rule? And have you ever tried to climb in another way?
