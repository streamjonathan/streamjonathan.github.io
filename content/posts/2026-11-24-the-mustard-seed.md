Small beginnings can feel like nothing at all.

"The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed," Jesus said. It is the smallest of all seeds. A man took it and sowed it in his field. But when it grew, it was larger than all the garden plants. It became a tree — so big that the birds of the air came and made their nests in its branches.

That is what God's kingdom is like. It starts smaller than you can see. And it grows into something that gives shelter to others.

What small thing in your life might grow bigger than you expect? Where have you seen something small become a shelter for others? And what would it mean that God's kingdom starts this small?
