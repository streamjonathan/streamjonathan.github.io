That’s My Mum May 8, 2014 [Jonathan McCallum](http://www.jonathanmccallum.com/author/jonathanmccallum/) [Jonathan](http://www.jonathanmccallum.com/category/jonathan/) 0
That’s my mum. 
 I found this photo of her, loose in a cupboard, 
 Ruffled on the corners. 
 It’s obvious she’s the focus. 
 I love to tell her that she’s at the centre of Jesus’ lens. 
 She feels a bit ruffled by that. 
 That image is still developing in her heart.
Mum, alongside Dad, was the loudest cheerer at the soccer field, the first to challenge the referee on a bad call, the first to comfort us when we fall.Her mum, too, was outspokenly loving with a keen eye on protecting and, when necessary, correcting anyone who forgot her girl has a mum. After some unprofessional comments at my mum’s new bank job her mum stormed into the office, uninvited, yet unmistakably understood: 
 Ladies were to be treated as ladies. 
 The boss went red, his character improved. 
 One mother changed the world a little.Mary was another favoured one, ruffled by God’s entrusting her with Jesus, His very Self. 
 She stormed an unwelcoming world, with a song, a son. 
 She scandalously protected the deity-babe, improving the world with her mother-love. 
 She changed the world believing that she was a favoured mum. 
 I’m not surprised that among Jesus’ last words before death and life again were: 
 “That’s my mum. Take care of her.” *Happy Mothers’ Day, mums.* 
 *You are favoured.* 
 *You are the centre of Jesus’ photo.* 
 *—*

 Sydney, Australia, circa 1955 
 After returning from WWII my grandfather worked for Kodak in Sydney, Australia. He took this photo of his daughter on her 5th birthday. My grandmother made the party hats, bought the straws and fizzy drink, and mixed and baked the cake to celebrate my mum’s early life.
