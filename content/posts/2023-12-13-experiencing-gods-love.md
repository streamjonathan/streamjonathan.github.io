Belief in God's love. What does that look like? I want to talk about how we respond to God's love. What is our role, what is our responsibility, in receiving it?

Over the years I have discovered that it is actually a step of forward movement toward faith, toward a sense of trust. As I step out in that small amount of faith, there is a gentleness and a warmth and an encouragement that I feel. It changes my perspective on how I see myself and how I see others.

![Jonathan talking about experiencing God's love](/images/experiencing-gods-love.jpg)

Often the weakness we have can be transformed into strength. Look at some of the people who follow Jesus very closely, in their love and adoration and worship of him, and their love for others. They were not people who were of high esteem in the society they came from. They were liberated and freed and transformed. Not without failure, and ups and downs and bumps along the way. Look at Peter. Look at the people who followed Jesus, both men and women. Their backgrounds are fascinating to see, and how God's love transforms us.

So that is the question I have for myself today. Do I believe that God loves me? Am I stepping forward into that, whether I feel like it or not? And what are some of the steps I can take?

Before I give some suggestions about spiritual practices, I think it is important to mention that this is an opportunity we have today. The light might be shining into your room right now. Being close to God might mean thinking about his love, or thinking about a scripture or a song. It might also mean calling up a friend and talking to them. There are many different ways to encourage both ourselves and others.

I think it is often forgotten how important it is to focus on encouraging others, and how much that actually encourages us in our walk with Jesus. God's love is here in this moment.
