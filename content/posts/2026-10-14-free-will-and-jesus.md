How long could we talk about the mystery of free will? God is above all, knowing all, all powerful, and somehow allowing things to happen that we don't want to happen. The more I went along in this video, the more I realised the layers and depths of it.

I want to start with this. Good people suffer. People suffer, and I do not know why. Maybe you have a complex answer or a simple one. My hope is that we, as followers of Jesus, wouldn't be quick to give answers that are going to be very harmful for someone to hear in the middle of suffering. I think we only learn that truth when we suffer ourselves.

Terrible things happen to people of all beliefs, all ages, cultures, places and times. That is important to say before we speak about free will.

![Jonathan talking about free will and Jesus](/images/free-will-and-jesus.jpg)

Forgive me for making this personal. I began following Jesus because I had nightmares where I felt very afraid, and I called out, Jesus, please help me. I experienced a deep freedom. I hope to express it in hundreds of videos. What matters is telling my story and reflecting on what God has been doing in my life.

I am not saying followers of Jesus escape suffering. God is holding us, and we are also walking and having responsibility. There is a deep mystery there, and grace in it. I don't think even the greatest theologians can claim to have understood that mystery of freedom, or God's ability to know and be all, and hold all time and space in his grasp.

So my thought is this. Each day you wake up, you have an opportunity to walk in that love and that freedom you have in Christ. Jesus has set us free from the powers that would control us, and from sin and death.
