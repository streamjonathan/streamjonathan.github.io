The power of water, from river and stream to estuary and bay finding a path to oceans. liquid life climbing back up in clouds or springing up from underground fountains make me think of the beauty and complexity of life.

NFT are intricate vehicles carrying beauty with fluidity around the world. Starting from an artist in a corner of the world passing from collector to collector. The format and style can vary and be as diverse as the imagination. Photography, digital art and assets tied to physical objects or maybe rent or land ownership contracts sent out to be validated on the blockchain instead of through traditional legal and business avenues.
