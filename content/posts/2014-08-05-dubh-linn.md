Stream, run, 
 Tears, years, joy of life. 
 Refresh, nourish, 
 Splattering rain. 
 Dubh Linn, Ireland. 
 Past King Sitric Silkenbeard’s stone idea, crowds come and go. 
 Across shimmering An Life River, people flow. 
 Yet I see in Your eyes the gift of life, like An Life. 
 Travelling from the Black Pool to the Sea. 
 Where we shall forever be.
