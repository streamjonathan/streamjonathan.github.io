exploring coves of basque culture, tables filled with people, fish, wine, bread

relaxing beside fire glistening friendship, shower of sun

riding waves on frosty shore, snow-iced hills above green freedom-hungry coast

hearing flowing rhythm from hearts poured out, jesus found afresh, friend of religion rejects, those despised or forgotten

leaving barefoot prints in soft earth common ground
