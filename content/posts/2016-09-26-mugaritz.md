San Sebastián-Donostia is home to many Michelin stars, and if you are visiting the city with a view to the gastronomic, you have probably heard of the world’s 14th best restaurant with two Michelin stars: **[Mugaritz](http://www.sansebastianfood.com/en/food-tours-and-events/packages/www.mugaritz.com) .**

In which case, you’re going to want to check out the unique dining experience in a special package recently developed by San Sebastián Food and Mugaritz.

It is a lunch to remember. A world-class meal that will enrapture your taste buds and leave your memory swirling with sensations. But it’s much more than a meal. The door to the kitchen will be opened to you. You will go behind the scenes, donning a chef’s coat in a top-of-the-line **[Michelin Star Secrets class](http://www.sansebastianfood.com/en/cooking-school/half-day-classes/michelin-star-secrets-cooking-class/)** .  Juan Vargas, co-head chef of Mugaritz for five years, will lead you through the intricacies of preparing the courses, presenting them in style, and pairing them to perfection. You’ll leave the kitchen with recipe cards, a cookbook, and a pocket full of skills to take back to your own dining table.

And there’s only one class left this year…so be sure to contact [San Sebastián Food](http://www.sansebastianfood.com/en/contact) and reserve your place today!
