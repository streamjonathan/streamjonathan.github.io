We all know what it feels like to be told we are too small to matter.

People were bringing little children to Jesus for him to touch them, but the disciples rebuked them. When Jesus saw this, he was indignant. He said to them, "Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. Truly I tell you, anyone who will not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it."

Then he took the children in his arms, put his hands on them and blessed them.

He did not tell the children to grow up first. He did not tell them to understand first. He took them as they were — and said the kingdom belongs to people like them.

Why were the disciples turning the children away? What does it mean to receive the kingdom of God like a little child? And who do we keep away from Jesus today?
