John begins his gospel with a claim, not a birth story. "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

The Word made everything. Nothing was made without him.

Then John says the strangest thing of all: "The Word became flesh and dwelt among us."

God did not stay far away. He came and lived here, among us. We saw his glory — full of grace and truth. John the Baptist pointed to him and said, "Behold the Lamb of God."

He did not send a message. He came himself.

What does it mean that God came to live among us? If Jesus is God in flesh, what does that say about God? And what would it mean to receive him?
