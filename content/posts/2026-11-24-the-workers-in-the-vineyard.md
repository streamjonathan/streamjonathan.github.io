We all know what it feels like when something does not seem fair.

Jesus said the kingdom of heaven is like a master of a house who went out early in the morning to hire labourers for his vineyard. He agreed with them for a denarius a day, and sent them in.

He went out again at nine in the morning, at noon, and at three in the afternoon, and each time he found more men standing idle and sent them into the vineyard too, saying, "Whatever is right I will give you."

At five in the afternoon he went out and found others standing idle. He said, "Why do you stand here idle all day?" They said, "Because no one has hired us." He said, "You go into the vineyard too."

When evening came, the owner said to his foreman, "Call the labourers and pay them their wages, beginning with the last, up to the first." Those hired at five received a denarius each. When the first came, they thought they would receive more. But they also received a denarius each.

They grumbled. "These last worked only one hour, and you have made them equal to us who have borne the burden of the day and the scorching heat."

The master said, "Friend, I am doing you no wrong. Did you not agree with me for a denarius? Take what belongs to you and go. I choose to give to this last worker as I give to you. Am I not allowed to do what I choose with what belongs to me? Or do you begrudge my generosity?"

Jesus finished, "So the last will be first, and the first last."

Does that sound fair to you? When have you felt like the worker hired first? And what does it say about God that he is generous like this?
