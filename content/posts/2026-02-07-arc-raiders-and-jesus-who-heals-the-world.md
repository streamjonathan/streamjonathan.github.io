I recorded this one while playing Arc Raiders, and I spoke with another player first. He had been playing for two weeks. I had been playing for two or three months. We wished each other a good day, and then I turned the microphone on and started talking.

Speaking about faith can feel like a very heavy topic. I want to be honest with you. I am not going to get it right. What I hope is that you hear that I love doing this, and that I love that Jesus is ministering and caring for us in the most intimate and careful way.

I am also aware that others will not like what I have to say, and that is really okay. I would even like to hear from people who disagree, or who have a different opinion, or people who agree and see it from another angle.

![The Arc Raiders logo on a building in a snowy landscape](/images/arc-raiders-and-jesus-who-heals-the-world.jpg)

As I watched the footage of the ice and the loneliness of the world in the game, I thought there is definitely a spiritual element to the way humanity feels lost and lonely without the connection to God, without that closeness.

And thankfully, we can return to the Creator. Or you could say the Creator has returned to us. Jesus walked the earth. He lived a life far beyond anything we could imagine, a perfect life. We see it in the way he treated people, how he loved, how his unconditional love was displayed for all. That includes the way he treated those who thought they had authority over others. Jesus saw his authority as love, even to lay down his life and die so we could find life.

This is a long redeeming story. It replaces the brokenness and the loneliness. Jesus brings healing and hope, and he gives life, life eternal.
