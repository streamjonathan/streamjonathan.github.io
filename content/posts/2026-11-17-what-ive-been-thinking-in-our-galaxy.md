Sitting at home in Europe, looking back at this note I wrote in a US carpark a few months ago.

I somehow am thinking of milky-galactic prose in an American parking lot, where I notice a lone vessel with brown liquid left to sunbathe alone.

"Milky" because I'm almost constantly sipping (with loud noises) milkshakes, or in this moment, a Starbucks. A family member gave us some gift vouchers, which is good because 14 US dollars still seems a bit too much for two cups of mostly (delicious) ice, syrup, and cow maternal flow. (Sorry, it made me laugh thinking in these udderly silly terms.)

Today, I find my mind is cloudily thankful. I've been in a state of travel, socializing, learning, meeting, and greeting for a month. It's the first day of unscheduled plans, so I took the chance to start writing again.

Did you hear about my time in Kyoto, Japan? I got to learn about the 26 Martyrs Christian Pilgrimage. It kind of altered my universe and I've been sharing with people about it.

Thanks for saying hi and many thanks for reading (back in the day when I went to Toastmasters people said not to thank after saying something in a speech, yet I figured that is kind of assuming I have something important to say which in your opinion might NOT be the case)—thank you for reading!
