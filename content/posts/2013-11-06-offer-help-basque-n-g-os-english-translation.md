An offer:

My name is Jonathan McCallum, my wife Sarah and I live here in The Basque Country.  We are interested in many cultures.  Our family is Australian-American, we have a daughter that speaks Basque, Spanish and English with a mixture of Aussie-American words, and our friends and relatives are scattered around the globe.  We have been living overseas for years and have travelled what some people would call “extensively” yet we would still love to experience more of this thing called “earth”.

Furthermore, we are in the process of adopting internationally and have been learning about the situation of children worldwide over the past 10 years.  We both have learned a lot from books and training related to “Children in Crisis” yet most of our interest and desire to help children flows from what we have seen and observed from living in different countries.

Our belief in and experience as followers of Jesus also influences who we are and what we do.  We’re not interested in religion, but as we have experienced Jesus relationally, we love to help others learn and experience more of who Jesus is.  And we find that a care and love for the world’s people grows deeper and stronger in us as we follow Jesus.

Adoption is a way we feel that our family can help, however we would also like to collaborate in sharing information with people that is broader and gives opportunities to help at many levels.

So, the offer:  We would love to network with Basque and European organizations that are already working to help promote the needs of children worldwide.  We can help with English, publicity, writing, etc.  Do you know an organization we can connect with?
