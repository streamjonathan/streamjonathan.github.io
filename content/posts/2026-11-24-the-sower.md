Some things we hear seem to go in one ear and out the other.

A sower went out to sow. As he scattered the seed, some fell on the path, and the birds came and ate it. Some fell on rocky ground, where there was not much soil. It sprang up quickly, but when the sun rose it withered, because it had no root. Some fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it. But some fell on good soil, and it grew and gave grain — a hundred, sixty, thirty times what was sown.

"Whoever has ears," Jesus said, "let them hear."

Which kind of ground are you right now? What is choking the seed in your life? And what would good soil look like for you?
