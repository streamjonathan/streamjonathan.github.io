I especially like the part in the song where it talks about being close to heaven. What is the person? I first heard this song at a youth group in Sydney, in my high school years, and the youth pastor used it as an example of what people are going through.

It summarised how I was feeling as a teenager. I was out and about on my skateboard, and at the youth group everyone was skating, and people were very friendly.

People were welcoming, and not in a religious way. They asked questions about my life and took time not to interrupt. I think they were intensely focused on the love of Jesus, and that was the mark of why these people were different.

![Jonathan listening with headphones while a drummer plays below](/images/heaven-is-possible-because-of-jesus.jpg)

Decades later, this song still stands out. I remember walking with the youth pastor and telling him I often wonder about all the life that happens internally, like thinking, and I don't know what to do with the thoughts that are negative and don't seem calm.

He taught me a simple story that stayed with me. A bird was flying over someone's head. You can't really stop the bird flying over. But as soon as it started to nest, you could make sure it didn't do that in your head, in your hair.

That wisdom has stayed with me for the rest of my life. It is pretty helpful, especially as a young person, when everything is being absorbed and you want a piece of calm inside.

I try to remember how much that group loved Jesus. It had a huge impact on me. Especially the part in the song about being close to heaven because of a person. I wonder how many people realise that person is God, that person is Jesus, and that heaven is very much attainable because of him.
