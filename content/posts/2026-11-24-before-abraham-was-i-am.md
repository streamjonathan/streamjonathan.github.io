Jesus was talking with the religious leaders in the temple. They asked him who he thought he was. They already knew his family. They knew where he came from. How could he speak like this?

Jesus said, "Before Abraham was, I am."

Abraham lived two thousand years before Jesus. But Jesus did not say, "I was there before Abraham." He said, "I am." Those are the words God spoke to Moses at the burning bush: "I AM WHO I AM."

The leaders understood exactly what he meant. They picked up stones to throw at him. But Jesus slipped away from them.

He did not back down. He did not explain it away. He said who he was, plainly.

What do you think he meant?
