I only recently began watching the Reimagined series–yes, I know, very late to the game–and I’m about half-way through. And it has been a riveting adventure that takes me back to one of my favourite genres of books and movies.

Below you can see my top five Battlestar Galactica characters. Each character is launched into a universe of rapidly paced drama delving into the essence of what it means to be human, where politically astute, split-second decisions will preserve or destroy life, and the fleet flees from the Cylons. The viewer is faced with complex and fascinating topics such as the role of the military in a democracy, religion in society, and the immense force we humans (or Cylons) call love.

I have been living overseas for the last 11 years, and now living in the Basque Country of Europe I would love to see it translated into the Basque language. Are any Basque speakers interested in approaching BSG to submit a plan for subtitles or a translation into the Basque language?

If you are looking for an intense science fiction drama that brings the viewer into a world of questions about our humanity then look no further. And if you’re interested in discussing some those questions raised in this intriguing mini-series, leave a comment on my social media.
