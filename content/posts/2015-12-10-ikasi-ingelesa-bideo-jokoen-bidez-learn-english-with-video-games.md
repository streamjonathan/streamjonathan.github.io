Learn English with me here in the Basque Country. I offer free conversational English lessons using video games while also talking about the safe use of social media and the internet. 
 This is a service that I have just launched and is currently offered for free within the Basque Country. 
 If you or your school are interested in having me teach conversational English at your school or group please contact me via the below email or via social media.

“Ikasi ingelesa bideo-jokoen bidez” is Basque for “Learn English with Video Games”. These lessons are all about having fun while perfecting and improving your spoken English. For suggested videos or to arrange for me to visit your school email me: jonathan @ refreshleaders.org (no spaces) or visit my social media & website links:
