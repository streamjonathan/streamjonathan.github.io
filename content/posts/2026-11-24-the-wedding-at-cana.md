We all know what it feels like when something runs out at the worst moment.

There was a marriage in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there. Jesus and his disciples were invited too.

When the wine ran out, his mother said to him, "They have no wine." Jesus said to her, "Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come."

His mother said to the servants, "Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it."

Six waterpots of stone stood there, for washing. Jesus said, "Fill the waterpots with water." They filled them to the brim. "Draw out now, and bear unto the governor of the feast." And they did.

When the governor tasted the water that was made wine, he said to the bridegroom, "Every man at the beginning doth set forth good wine; and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse: but thou hast kept the good wine until now."

This was the first miracle Jesus did, and his disciples believed on him.

Why do you think Jesus helped at a wedding? What do you make of his mother telling the servants to do whatever he said? And what does this first miracle say about him?
