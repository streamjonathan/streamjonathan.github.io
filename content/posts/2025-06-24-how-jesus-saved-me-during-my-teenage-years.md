I became a believer in Jesus when I was around fourteen years old, after years of things leading up to it.

There were dreams I had where I saw myself falling into an endless place. They frightened me. But I said, God, if you exist, please make yourself known to me. And I asked that same Jesus, would you take away these dreams, and would you give me sleep that is peaceful?

For a whole year, I had very restful sleep. I don't remember if I had a nightmare. And I remember that as a significant stepping stone.

At that time I also read the Chronicles of Narnia and started them, and I found them enthralling. A friend invited me to a youth group, and I went along with my skateboard under my arm. One of the youth pastors was sharing about Jesus, and he was such a good listener. He spoke about Jesus being God, and how he died and rose again for us, and has carried us into this new life that we get to live in him.

![Jonathan talking about his teenage years and coming to faith](/images/how-jesus-saved-me-during-my-teenage-years.jpg)

Those were amazing times in my life. The theological influence of the Chronicles of Narnia, and then later the other books C. S. Lewis wrote. Getting the New Testament for the first time and reading it, and then reading other portions of the scriptures, and having fellowship with others who knew and loved Jesus.

I think about this now when I look at how much is changing around us. There is a cycle of producing more with less effort, and I think that means less brain power and less critical thinking. People are training AI models in their voice to reply to messages and create videos. The original voice of humanity is, in my opinion, being lost. My story is mine, in my own voice, and I want to keep telling it that way.
