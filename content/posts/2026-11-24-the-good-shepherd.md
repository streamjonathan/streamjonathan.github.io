Jesus said, "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."

A hired man runs when the wolf comes. The sheep are not his, so why would he stay? But the shepherd stays. He knows his sheep, and his sheep know his voice. They follow him because they know the sound of it.

"I know my sheep," Jesus said, "and my sheep know me."

He did not say he watches the sheep from a distance. He said he knows them — and he would die for them.

What would it mean to be known like that? And what does it say that he would lay down his life for the sheep?
