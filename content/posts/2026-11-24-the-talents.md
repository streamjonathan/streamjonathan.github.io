We have all been given something — and the question is what we do with it.

A man about to travel called his servants and gave them his money: five talents to one, two to another, one to another — each according to his ability.

The first traded and made five more. The second made two more. But the third dug a hole and hid his talent in the ground.

When the master returned, he said to the first two, "Well done, good and faithful servant." But the third said, "I was afraid, so I hid your talent in the ground." The master answered, "You wicked and lazy servant. You should have put my money with the bankers." He took the talent away and gave it to the one who had ten. The servant who hid his talent was thrown out into the darkness.

"To everyone who has," Jesus said, "more will be given."

What have you been given? Are you using it, or burying it? And what would it look like to hear "well done"?
