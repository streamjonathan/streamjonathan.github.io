A woman came into the house while Jesus was at dinner.

One of the Pharisees, named Simon, invited Jesus to eat with him. Jesus went in and sat down.

Then a woman in the city, who was a sinner, came in with an alabaster box of ointment. She stood at his feet behind him, weeping, and washed his feet with her tears. She wiped them with the hairs of her head, kissed them, and anointed them with the ointment.

Simon thought to himself, if this man were a prophet, he would know what kind of woman this is.

Jesus told him of two men who owed money, one five hundred pence and one fifty. Neither could pay, and both were forgiven. "Which of them will love him most?" "The one forgiven most," Simon said. Jesus said he had judged rightly.

Then Jesus said to the woman, "Thy sins are forgiven." And to her, "Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace."

Why do you think the woman came to Jesus at all? What did Simon see that Jesus did not?
