We all know what it feels like to ask for help for someone we love.

When Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion came to him, asking for help. "Lord," he said, "my servant lies at home paralysed, suffering terribly."

Jesus said, "Shall I come and heal him?"

The centurion replied, "Lord, I do not deserve to have you come under my roof. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed. For I myself am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I tell this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and that one, 'Come,' and he comes."

When Jesus heard this, he was amazed. "Truly I tell you, I have not found anyone in Israel with such great faith," he said. "Many will come from the east and the west, and will take their places at the feast with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven."

Then Jesus said to the centurion, "Go! Let it be done just as you believed it would." And his servant was healed at that moment.

What did the centurion understand about authority? Why was Jesus amazed? And what would "just say the word" look like in your life?
