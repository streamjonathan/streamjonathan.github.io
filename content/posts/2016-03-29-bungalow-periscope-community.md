[Periscope](https://www.periscope.tv/) is live-streaming your story from a phone to the world. It turns out that this rapidly growing community values educational, fun, and engaging content. If you decide to join Periscope you will occasionally find [rude and disrespectful people](http://www.jonathanmccallum.com/2015/11/13/periscope-protocol-and-thoughts-from-amanda-oleander/) , however **I have found that the vast majority of Periscopers are authentic, uplifting and thoughtful** .

> A few months ago one of those people, [Lauren Coopersmith](https://twitter.com/lopaige24) from [Bungalow](http://www.hirebungalow.com/) , sent me a message after seeing my Periscope while I was surfing. Since then I decided to [take part in this great community.](https://twitter.com/hirebungalow)
