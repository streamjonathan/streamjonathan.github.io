We all know what it feels like to have a question we are almost afraid to ask.

There was a man named Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a ruler of the Jews. He came to Jesus by night and said, "Rabbi, we know that thou art a teacher come from God."

Jesus answered, "Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

Nicodemus said, "How can a man be born when he is old?" Jesus said a person must be born of water and of the Spirit. The wind blows where it wishes. You hear it, but you cannot tell where it comes from. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.

Then Jesus said, "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."

Why do you think Nicodemus came at night? What do you think it means to be born again? And what does it say that God loved the world this much?
