Bill and Ted face the possibility of failing history class, which could lead to Ted being sent to military school. Rufus, a wild guy from the future, arrives in a time-traveling phone booth and instructs them to collect historical figures for a presentation. The duo travels through time, encountering famous figures and having a blast while being cautious not to alter the past. Despite challenges, they successfully round up the historical figures for their presentation. In the end, they impress their classmates, avoid military school for Ted, and emerge triumphant. The adventure is a mix of history, friendship, and epic time-traveling escapades. Party on, dudes!
