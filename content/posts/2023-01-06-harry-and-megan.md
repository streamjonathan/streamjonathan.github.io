The Duke of Sussex mentions on Netflix that “Meghan and I met over instagram. “. According to Harry he was scrolling through his feed and saw a friend with Megan who was using a Snapchat lens with dog ears. He said: “That was the first thing – I was like ‘who is that?’

In November 2017, Prince Harry proposed to Megan in the privacy of their own home, and she said yes.

As they prepared to tie the knot, Prince Harry and Megan faced a number of challenges, including intense media scrutiny and criticism from some quarters. This controversy seems to of only increased in 2023 with the public divided about how they feel about them both.

Did they expect that on May 19, 2018 when they exchanged vows in a beautiful ceremony at St. George’s Chapel in Windsor Castle that their lives would turn out this way?

Prince Harry and Megan Markle seem committed to using their platform to make a positive impact on the world and yet the controversies and disagreements with the Royal family seem to be an ongoing topic.
