Eric Oliver for judge

March 1, 2014 by Jonathan McCallum

0

Some years back, on a dripping-hot Indiana day, I was introduced as “the Aussie” to Eric Oliver over a tennis net. Today, after rounds of tennis, up-and-down-the-creek kayaking, and plenty of chats at Courthouse Grounds, Eric Oliver is my trusted friend. As a “naturalized” citizen of the USA, I have had to work at becoming “American” by listening, studying, and coming to love this vibrant culture. Eric has displayed a depth of integrity, openness, and fairness that endeared me not just to him, but to America as a whole. It is people like Eric who make the world (my world) a different, better place. His listening heart, sharp mind and ability to see things clearly allow him to discern with a deep clarity that prompts trust in those with whom he interacts, endearing him to people from diverse political and religious backgrounds.

Eric Oliver
