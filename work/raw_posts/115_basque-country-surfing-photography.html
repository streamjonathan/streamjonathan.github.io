Basque Country Surfing Photography

By Jonathan McCallum July 28, 2015 Photography
  • No Comments
  • 0
  • 0





Tucked away along the coast of Gipuzkoa province in the Basque Country, the autumn sunlight streams down on the blue sea, the green txakoli-covered fields, and the sheep spotted hills, as a few pilgrims hike past with St. James sea shells on their back packs. Just in front of them bobbing up and down in the Atlantic are a few surfers enjoying the sea gifting them a few barrels to tuck into.
