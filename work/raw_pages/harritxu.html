Below is a sketch of my rock pad and phone named “Harritxu”. I carry it around as a visual reminder to focus, to slow down, to be completely present with the person next to me, to listen, to care, to love, Harritxu means “Pebble” in Basque. People are poetry, live is brief, I hope to enjoy every moment as life streams past here on the beautiful Basque coast.
