Laura

What makes you laugh?
Usually comical situations that happen in day-to-day life, really good jokes, I guess my genuine laughs come from situations where I am with people I know really well and just a look at each other and the “non spoken” comments a look can carry make me laugh.

What makes you cry?
Emotional happenings, whether it is in my life or in a movie, good or bad, crying seems the easiest way to express my feelings. When I am too happy or really angry, I tend to cry.

What do you like about Jesus?
To me Jesus is an entity I can rely on when I don’t have any rational reasons to convince myself that “everything is going to be ok” or that “everything is possible”. I don’t have any specific belief so what I like about Jesus is that it’s a name that itself stands for a great man that really existed but it also stands for that entity that makes you push beyond your limits, stay strong when there apparently no reason or just feel accompanied when you’re alone.
